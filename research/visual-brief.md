## 共同画风

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: editorial illustration for an interactive Chinese engineering article. Ink and muted gouache on warm cream paper (#f7f4ec), precise navy outlines (#2c4a7c), restrained burnt orange (#a95f16), soft teal accents, visible fine paper grain, an editorial field-notes sensibility. The same small practical studio desk, natural materials, grounded objects, calm light. Landscape 3:2. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no glossy 3D, no generic glowing AI brain. Illustrations are conceptual, not screenshots or measured evidence. Keep all main objects within the central 80% of the image, leave room between objects. All individual images should share this visual language.

## 01 · 三种入口

Primary request: A top-down view of one small illustration studio desk. An unfinished botanical drawing lies at the center. Three distinct practical ways of reaching the same task sit around it: a terminal-like mechanical keypad, an open step-by-step notebook with simple pictograms, and a neat modular cable connector next to the notebook. Three separate thin colored cords reach the same drawing holder, clearly distinct and never tangled. The notebook does not depend physically on the connector; each route remains visibly separate. The image should communicate multiple ways to carry out the same work, not a hierarchy of intelligence. Use the shared illustration style. No readable text anywhere.

## 02 · 按需打开

No readable text, no lettering.

Communicate that a watcher timing out does not mean the work stopped. Use the shared illustration style. No readable text or numbers on the ticket or clock.
